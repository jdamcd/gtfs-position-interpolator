Vehicle positions

This is a demo of a tiny server that interpolates missing vehicle positions in GTFS-RT feeds, using trip updates and static GTFS schedules. It was made by Jamie McDonald at the Recurse Center (W1'26).

Vehicles are placed along their route between the previous stop and the next, timed to arrive when predicted, and will speed up or slow down when the prediction changes.
